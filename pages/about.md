Hi I am **{{ site.author.name }}** :wave:,<br>
I am currently a MSc Student at Seoul National University, Seoul, South Korea since 2024 as a [BK21 FOUR Fellow](/assets/BK21FOUR_Fellowship.pdf) by the Korean government.

I am currently nominated as a **Fulbright Scholar for the graduate studies in the upcoming semester (Fall, 2026).**

My research interest is adaptive intelligence in robotics, especially controlling robots using learning based approaches for real assistant in daily life.
