## Intern at Hyundai Motors Group Headquarters
### ㅤ
From July to October 2022, I was a student intern at Hyundai Motors Group headquarters in Yangjae, Seoul. On the S Innovation Progress Team in the Robotics Division, I supported robotics technology development, startup analysis and patent assetization for smart factory planning.

I worked on the robot software architecture for HMGICS (Hyundai Motor Group Innovation Center Singapore), Hyundai's smart factory in Singapore, and researched robot automation for smart factories. I also reviewed applicants for the 2022 HMG ZER01NE Accelerator robotics startup program, wrote weekly reports on innovation and technology trends for the team, and translated documents and meetings for international advisory groups.
