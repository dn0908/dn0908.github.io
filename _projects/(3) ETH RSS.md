## ETH Zurich Robotics Summer School
### ㅤ

In June 2025, I took part in the ETH Zurich Robotics Summer School in Epeisses, Switzerland, with a fellowship from the ETH RobotX initiative.


<img src="/assets/img/teamfour.png" alt="Four-now-Team" style="width:100%;">


It was an intense week covering state estimation, localization and mapping, path planning and exploration, camera modeling, and more. We applied these skills in a search-and-rescue challenge, where our robot had to navigate autonomously, explore an unknown environment and detect objects.
