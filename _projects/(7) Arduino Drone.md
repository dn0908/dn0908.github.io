## Arduino-based Quadcopter Project
### ㅤ
I built an Arduino-based quadcopter running the open-source MultiWii flight software, and tuned its PID loops for stable hovering.

**Hardware:**
- Flight controller: Arduino Nano with MultiWii
- Frame: QAV250
- Sensors: GY-86 IMU (MPU6050), GPS module
- Propulsion: BLDC motors with Emax ESCs
- Radio: Spektrum DX6e
